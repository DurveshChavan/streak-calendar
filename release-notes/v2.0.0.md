The first full release of Streak Calendar. Download **StreakCalendar-v2.0.0.apk** below and open it on your phone.

### New
- **Widget settings:** long-press the widget → Settings for transparency (0–100 %), light / dark / match app, and which habit it shows, with a live preview over your wallpaper.
- **Switch habits on the widget:** tap its streak badge.
- **Full-page 4x6 widget:** adds this month's done, missed and best streak, plus the next holidays. Both sizes now show a proper preview when you add them.
- **Swipe through months:** a long swipe across the calendar changes month; a short swipe on a day still marks it.
- **Backup & restore:** copy all your habits, marks and notes as text and paste them back on this or any phone.
- **New app icon**, splash screen and themed icon.
- Works in **split screen**, **pop-up view** and landscape.

### Fixed
- Tapping a day opened its details and closed them again at once; only a long press worked.
- Switching between light and dark lagged.
- The keyboard covered the habit name field.
- The back button now closes an open sheet instead of leaving the app.
- Notes now save while you type.

### Updating
From this release on, every update installs over the previous one and keeps your data.

**Coming from the 1.0 test build?** Android has to remove 1.0 before installing 2.0, and that clears 1.0's data. To keep it, copy it out first:
1. Connect the phone to a computer by USB (USB debugging on) and open Streak Calendar 1.0.
2. In Chrome on the computer, go to `chrome://inspect/#devices` and click **inspect** under *WebView in app.streakcalendar*.
3. In the **Console** tab, run `copy(localStorage.getItem('streakcal.v2'))`. Your data is now on the computer's clipboard; paste it into a note or an email to yourself.
4. Uninstall 1.0, install 2.0, then tap the streak badge → **Restore…**, paste it and tap **Restore**.
