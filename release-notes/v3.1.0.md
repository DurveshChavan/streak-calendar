Streak Calendar 3.1. Download **StreakCalendar-v3.1.0.apk** below and open it on your phone. It installs over 2.0 and keeps all your habits, marks and notes.

### New
- **Four kinds of habit:** *Every day*, *Some days a week* (a weekly goal like 3 of 7), *Break a habit* (count the urges you resist, with an optional weekly budget) and *Track a number* (pages, minutes, glasses, with an optional weekly target).
- **Progress that survives a miss:** the badge now shows your **total** in big numbers, with the streak or this week's progress underneath. Only a day you mark as missed breaks a streak; a blank day never does.
- **Today's score:** "1 / 3" of your every-day habits done today.
- **Timers:** a *Start* timer to get going, or a *Delay* timer to wait out an urge. It runs with the app closed, shows a countdown notification with a Stop button, and counts on the day it started.
- **Habit details:** total, this week, streak and an 8-week trend.
- **Why it was missed:** add a short reason to a missed day.
- **Gentle nudges:** a short start the day after a miss, a check-in after two misses in a row, and a weekly review on the last day of the week. You can turn them off.
- **Later and Avoid:** park a habit without losing its data, and keep a list of things to avoid with what to do instead.
- **Week starts on any day** you choose.
- **New action widget:** one habit, its total and this week, and a button that starts its timer from the home screen.
- **Refreshed look:** frosted glass tiles, soft colour glows, Roboto, and missed days in calm grey instead of red, in light and dark.
- **Accessibility:** stronger contrast on done days, and every sheet works with a keyboard (focus moves in, Escape closes it).

### Fixed
- A widget timer stopped after it had already ended no longer loses its count.
- A reason typed just before closing a sheet is now saved.

### Updating
- **From 2.0:** install over it. Your habits become *Every day* habits and nothing is lost.
- **Own sync server:** update the server to 3.1 too. Until you do, 3.1 shows "Sync: the server needs an update" and keeps everything on the phone. A 2.0 phone talking to an updated server shows "Sync failed" until it is updated as well.
- Make a backup first if you like: tap the badge → **Copy backup**.
